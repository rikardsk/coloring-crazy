# ColoringCrazy - Feature Ideas & Roadmap

This document captures potential features and enhancements for future development.

---

## 7. 💬 Speech Bubbles & Text Callouts (In Progress)
- **Speech & Thought Bubbles**: Add customizable comic speech bubbles, thought clouds, shout/action stars, and caption boxes to line art pages.
- **Interactive Tail & Positioning**: Drag to move, resize, rotate, and adjust the tail pointer pointing to characters.
- **Custom Styling**: Choose custom text, fonts, colors, border thickness, and fill backgrounds.

---

## 1. 🎨 Gradients & Pattern Fill Mode
- **Gradient Flood Fill**: Fill enclosed line-art regions with linear or radial two-color gradients (e.g., sunset blend, rainbow, pastel glow).
- **Textures & Shading**: Paint or fill with soft glitter, watercolor texture overlays, or paper grain for added artistic depth.

---

## 2. ☸️ Radial & Symmetry Drawing Tool
- **Live Mirror Drawing**: Enable horizontal, vertical, or 4 to 12-spoke radial symmetry.
- **Custom Mandala Creation**: Allows users to draw their own intricate line art mandalas and geometric patterns directly inside the studio.

---

## 3. 🖼️ Interactive "Before / After" Split Slider
- **Masterpiece Showcase**: In the My Masterpieces section, add a sliding divider overlay so users can drag back and forth to compare the original uncolored line art side-by-side with their completed colored artwork.

---

## 4. 🎵 Ambient Relaxing Soundscapes
- **Studio Atmosphere**: Add an optional sound player in the Coloring Studio with soothing background audio presets (gentle rain, cozy fireplace, lofi beats, forest birds, ocean waves).

---

## 5. 📚 Multi-Page PDF Coloring Book Generator
- **Printable Books**: Select multiple line art pages from the library and generate a downloadable, printable PDF coloring book with a custom cover page, title, and page layout.

---

## 6. 🎬 Timelapse / Step-by-step Replay
- **Coloring Process Recording**: Capture stroke & fill history to replay the coloring process as a short animation or export as an animated GIF/MP4 video.
